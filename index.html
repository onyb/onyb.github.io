Accomplishing…

I'm a Senior Staff Engineer at Brave, working on web3 initiatives, mostly Wallet. I like building in public, and much of what I do is open source.

I work where product strategy becomes code, and I spend about as much time on what to build as on how to build it. The work I'm proudest of is rarely the clever kind. It's usually small, quietly high leverage, and pointed at a real person on the other side of the screen.

Outside Brave, I think a lot about LLMs, markets, and the craft of building startups from zero. I run an engineering and consulting practice through 21M Labs.

I started my career in Paris and spent several good years there before trading the Seine for Bangalore, where I'm based now.
